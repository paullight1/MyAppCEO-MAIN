# CEO Control Hub: Operating Your App Guide

Welcome to the **CEO Control Hub**. This guide explains how to use the marketplace tools to manage your app's lifecycle, from initial growth to a successful exit.

## 1. Connecting Your App
To unlock CEO Control, you must first connect your app's telemetry and financials.
1. **API Keys:** Generate a "CEO Control Key" in your MVPLab dashboard.
2. **SDK Integration:** Add `@mvplab/sdk` to your project's `dependencies`.
3. **Financial Link:** Connect your Stripe or App Store account via the **Connections** tab.

---

## 2. Operating Your App
CEO Control gives you "remote pilot" capabilities.
- **Maintenance Mode:** Toggle a global maintenance screen if you're fixing a critical bug.
- **Dynamic Pricing:** Update subscription tiers or feature access without a code push.
- **Broadcast Messages:** Send in-app announcements or push notifications to all users.

---

## 3. Monitoring Revenue & Performance
The **Financial Command Center** provides a single source of truth:
- **Real-Time Revenue:** Watch your MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue) grow with live updates.
- **User Retention:** Analyze which features keep users coming back.
- **Worth Indicator:** See how your "App Worth" changes as you hit new revenue milestones.

---

## 4. Growth & Marketing Decisions
Decide how to scale your user base:
- **Link Social Media:** Connect TikTok and Instagram to see how social engagement correlates with app downloads.
- **UGC (User Generated Content):** Use the built-in marketplace to hire creators to make videos for your app.
- **In-House Campaigns:** Launch referral programs or discount codes from the dashboard.

---

## 5. Exit Strategy: Selling Your App
When you're ready to move on to your next project:
- **Marketplace Listing:** Put your app on the marketplace with a pre-verified revenue history.
- **Selling Stakes:** Need capital but don't want to sell the whole thing? Sell a 10% or 20% stake to investors.
- **Negotiating Offers:** Manage buyer inquiries and secure the best price for your hard work.

---

## 6. Best Practices for CEOs
- **Daily Review:** Check your **CEO Dashboard** every morning to see the last 24h of growth.
- **Data-Driven Moves:** Use the **A/B Testing** tools before making major pricing changes.
- **Security:** Never share your CEO Control Key; rotate it immediately if it's compromised.
