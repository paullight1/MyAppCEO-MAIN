# CEO Social & UGC Hub: Automation & Growth Guide

This guide details how to leverage the **Social Hub** to automate your app's social media engagement and hire top-tier UGC creators from the **@talent_mvplab** community.

## 1. Social Media Automation (ManyChat Style)
Automate your DMs and comments to convert followers into users without manual effort.

### How to Set Up:
1. **Link Accounts:** Go to the **Social Hub** and connect your X (Twitter), Instagram, or TikTok account.
2. **Create Rules:** Define triggers (keywords) and actions (auto-replies).
   - *Example:* Trigger: `Keyword "beta"` → Action: `Send DM with Invite Link`.
3. **Monitor Performance:** Track how many "hits" each rule receives and optimize your copy.

### Automation Best Practices:
- **Be Human:** Even automated replies should sound personal and helpful.
- **Fast Response:** Set up "Demo" keywords to immediately send scheduling links to high-intent leads.
- **Admin Notifications:** For complex questions, set the action to `Notify CEO` so you can jump in manually.

---

## 2. Hiring UGC Talent (@talent_mvplab)
Scale your app's reach by hiring vetted creators who specialize in SaaS and consumer apps.

### The Hiring Process:
1. **Browse Talent:** View the curated list of creators in the **Social Hub**.
2. **Review Portfolios:** Check ratings, follower counts, and previous UGC work.
3. **Send Hire Request:**
   - Define your **Proposal** (what you need: e.g., "15s TikTok Review").
   - Set your **Budget**.
   - Link to a specific **Campaign** if you have one active.
4. **Escrow Protection:** Once the talent accepts, your budget is held in escrow until the video is delivered and approved.

---

## 3. Integrated Growth Workflow
1. **Identify Need:** Your dashboard shows a dip in user downloads.
2. **Hire Talent:** Request a UGC "How-to" video from a tech creator.
3. **Automate Engagement:** Set up a rule: `Keyword "video"` → `Reply with App Store Link`.
4. **Track Results:** Watch your "Downloads" metric in the CEO Dashboard tick upward.

---

## 4. Security & Permissions
- **Encrypted Tokens:** Social media access tokens are encrypted at rest and never exposed.
- **Revoke Anytime:** You can disconnect any platform or pause any automation rule instantly.
- **Vetted Talent:** Every creator in the `@talent_mvplab` pool has undergone identity and quality verification.
