# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Two equally-weighted sides of a marketplace:

- **App founders** — indie developers and small teams who list their apps, run investment campaigns, manage equity/cap tables, invite cofounders and team members, and track app performance. Context: working sessions at a desk, returning several times a week.
- **Investors** — people browsing listings and campaigns to buy apps or invest in them, tracking a portfolio and watchlist. Context: evaluative browsing, comparing opportunities, needing trust signals.

## Product Purpose

MyAppCEO (MVPLAB marketplace) is a platform where app founders operate their app businesses end-to-end — from idea, to listed product, to fundraising, to team and legal management — and where investors discover and fund those apps. Success: a founder can take an app from onboarding to a live, credible listing/campaign without dead ends; an investor can evaluate and act with confidence.

## Brand Personality

Apple-adjacent calm confidence: clean, precise, trustworthy. Brand blue #0071e3 as the single accent. The interface should feel like serious financial/product infrastructure, not a hackathon demo.

## Anti-references

- Hackathon-demo feel: dead buttons, "coming soon" stubs, fabricated numbers presented as real.
- Generic SaaS clichés: gradient-text heroes, identical card grids, hero-metric templates.
- Crypto-hype aesthetics for the investment surfaces — trust over excitement.

## Design Principles

1. **No dead ends** — every card, button, and nav item leads somewhere real; every flow can complete or explain why not.
2. **Honest data** — real data or clearly-labeled sample data; never fabricated metrics presented as live.
3. **Optional depth** — power features (equity, legal, campaigns) never block the simple path (list an app).
4. **Trust by detail** — the investment side must read as diligent: verified badges, provenance of numbers, consistent states.
5. **One system** — shared tokens, shared status/badge vocabulary across ideas → apps → listings → campaigns.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA target: 4.5:1 body contrast, keyboard-reachable interactive elements, reduced-motion alternatives for framer-motion animations, dark mode parity (existing token system).
