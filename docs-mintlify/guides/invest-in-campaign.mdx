# Invest in a Campaign

Invest in app campaigns and earn ROI from app revenue.

## Prerequisites

- Verified MyAppCEO account
- Funds in wallet (minimum $100)
- Completed investor profile

## Steps

### 1. Browse campaigns

Navigate to `/campaigns` to view active campaigns.

Filter by:
- Category (SaaS, Mobile, API)
- Funding progress
- Minimum investment
- ROI potential

### 2. Evaluate a campaign

Before investing, review:

| Metric | What to check |
|--------|---------------|
| Revenue | Verified monthly revenue |
| Growth | Month-over-month growth |
| Churn | User retention rate |
| Team | Founder experience |
| Terms | ROI schedule, exit clauses |

### 3. View campaign details

Click on a campaign to see:
- App metrics and charts
- Use of funds breakdown
- Investment tiers
- Investor Q&A

### 4. Click Invest

Select investment amount:

```typescript
// Investment calculation
const investment = {
  campaign_id: 'uuid',
  amount: 500,
  tier: 'Silver'
}

// Stake received
const stake = (amount / goal_amount) * max_stake
// Example: (500 / 100000) * 20 = 0.1%
```

### 5. Confirm payment

1. Review investment summary
2. Confirm payment source
3. Click **Confirm Investment**

Funds are held in escrow until campaign ends.

### 6. Track your investment

View at `/my-investments`:
- Investment status
- Stake percentage
- Expected ROI
- Campaign progress

## Code example

```typescript
// Invest via API
const investment = await fetch('/api/campaigns/:id/invest', {
  method: 'POST',
  headers: {
    'Content-Type': 'application/json',
    'Authorization': `Bearer ${token}`
  },
  body: JSON.stringify({
    amount: 500,
    tier: 'Silver'
  })
})
```

## After campaign succeeds

When the campaign reaches its goal:

1. Your stake is confirmed
2. Investment status → `active`
3. You receive access to:
   - App performance dashboard
   - Monthly ROI reports
   - Investor updates

## After campaign fails

If the campaign doesn't reach its goal:

1. Funds automatically refunded
2. Investment status → `refunded`
3. Money returns to your wallet

## ROI distribution

You receive ROI based on:

```
Monthly ROI = App Revenue × Your Stake %
```

Example:
- App revenue: $10,000/month
- Your stake: 0.5%
- Your ROI: $50/month

### ROI schedule

| Day | Action |
|-----|--------|
| 1st | Revenue calculated |
| 5th | ROI distributed to wallet |

## Selling your stake

You can sell your stake on the secondary market:

1. Go to `/my-investments`
2. Select investment
3. Click **List for Sale**
4. Set your asking price

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| Insufficient funds | Add funds to wallet |
| Campaign not accepting | Check campaign status |
| Payment failed | Verify payment method |
