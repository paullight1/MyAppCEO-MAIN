## 2. Priority Issues

### [P0] Fabricated content in the seller flow
**What:** `CreateListingPage.tsx:620-652` ships a fake testimonial — "Sarah's Success Story… 3 offers within 48 hours… Final Sale ₦60,000,000 VERIFIED" — and `:1232-1235` an invented statistic ("Verified revenue streams increase buyer confidence by +85%").
**Why it matters:** this is a trust-critical financial product with a real revenue-verification system; a fabricated "VERIFIED" sale in the guide panel poisons the credibility of every genuine verified badge. A Stripe-fluent user spots it immediately.
**Fix:** delete both. If social proof is needed, show real aggregate platform stats or nothing.
**Command:** `/impeccable clarify CreateListingPage`

### [P0] Post-offer dead end (missing sent-offers view)
**What:** items #1 above.
**Fix:** add an "Offers" section to the buyer dashboard (or MyInvestments tab) consuming `getMySentOffers`, with status chips (pending/countered/accepted/rejected) and links into escrow when accepted.
**Command:** `/impeccable craft buyer offers view`

### [P0] Campaign share route 404 + visitor auth wall
**What:** items #2 and #3 above — the two public entry points into fundraising are broken (share) or blocked (auth gate).
**Fix:** add `/campaigns/share/:shareToken` route resolving via `getCampaignByShareToken`; make `/campaigns/:id` public read-only (gate only invest/actions), keeping `CampaignInvestPage` protected.
**Command:** `/impeccable harden campaign public access`

### [P1] Mock analytics presented as real
**What:** `AppDetailPage.tsx:26` `MOCK_ANALYTICS` renders as a live "Revenue Trend" chart (`:256, :439`) and "Active Users" StatTile (`:458`) with no sample badge — while sibling `AnalyticsPage.tsx:90` does this honestly with "Sample data" chips. Also `DashboardPage.tsx:351-360` marks "Set up your legal entity" done using `campaignsCount > 0` as a proxy — fabricated completion.
**Fix:** copy the AnalyticsPage badge pattern; drive the legal-entity goal from actual legal-entity data (the backend exists). Decide `/app/:id`'s fate — it's also unreachable from any nav (wire it or remove it).
**Command:** `/impeccable harden AppDetailPage`

### [P1] Owner blindness on ListingDetailPage
**What:** item #4 above — owners see buyer CTAs on their own listing and can open the offer modal on themselves.
**Fix:** `isOwner = listing.sellerId === user?.id` → swap CTA block for "This is your listing — Edit · Manage · View offers" banner.
**Command:** `/impeccable harden ListingDetailPage owner mode`

### [P1] Error-state gaps and raw error passthrough
**What:** ManageListingsPage has no error state at all (fetch failure silently shows empty); DashboardPage swallows 4 catch blocks; `AdminReviewQueuePage.tsx:473/491/515/552` shows unguarded `err.message`; ~30 more files surface raw exception text.
**Fix:** ErrorState + retry on ManageListings/Dashboard; central error-message mapper (the friendly-classification pattern already exists in `AppOnboardingWizard`'s `FRIENDLY_SUBMIT_ERRORS` — generalize it).
**Command:** `/impeccable harden error states`

### [P1] Wizard work lost on refresh
**What:** AppOnboardingWizard (4 steps), CreateIdeaPage (4 steps), CreateCampaignPage hold state in `useState` only — refresh mid-flow loses everything. Only CreateListingPage has Save Draft (manual, no autosave, no unsaved-changes warning).
**Fix:** sessionStorage autosave keyed per wizard (the invest flow already round-trips sessionStorage — `CampaignInvestPage.tsx:203` — reuse the pattern).
**Command:** `/impeccable harden wizard drafts`

### [P2] "One system" fragmentation
Two PRD systems (unlinked), two team-management surfaces (AppDashboard Team tab's `AddTeamMemberModal` vs TeamPage's `CofounderInviteModal`), two onboarding start grids (DashboardPage first-run vs AppIntakePage), `window.confirm` in the idea flow (`IdeaDetailPage.tsx:203-251`) while `ConfirmDialog` is standard elsewhere, three badge components + ~33 ad-hoc inline badges, ₦ hardcoded (`OfferModal.tsx:39`, CreateListingPage) vs dynamic `formatCurrency` elsewhere, `text-primary` vs `text-accent` design languages between public and transaction pages.
**Command:** `/impeccable distill design system` then `/impeccable polish`

### [P2] Accessibility debt in modals
`ui/dialog.tsx:40-47` handles Esc but has no focus trap/restore; 5 hand-rolled modals (`DesignLightbox`, `PostUpdateModal`, `ShareIdeaModal`, `CofounderInviteModal`, `LicenseApplicationModal`) have neither Esc nor trap. Color-only status dots at `DesignStudioPage.tsx:558` and `StakeMonitorPage.tsx:223`. Icon-only unlabeled button `SocialHubLayout.tsx:437`.
**Command:** `/impeccable audit modals`

### [P2] Spinner-over-skeleton on data-heavy pages
DashboardPage, ListingDetailPage, AppDashboardPage, FinancesPage, MyIdeasPage use spinners; the skeleton pattern already exists (Browse/Blog/Campaign/Marketplace/Watchlist are exemplary). Also FinancesPage defaults to hardcoded `'Q1'` (`FinancesPage.tsx:103`) — stale in July.
**Command:** `/impeccable polish loading states`

## 5. Persona Red Flags

**Jordan (first-timer founder):** Signs up → "check your email" dead-end with no resend or "I've verified" path (`AuthPage.tsx:97-106`). Completes the 4-step app wizard → silently teleported to a dashboard with no confirmation of what was created (unreachable success screen). Meets **two** near-identical "start" grids (DashboardPage first-run + AppIntakePage) and can't tell them apart. Must decide marketing-notification preferences before being allowed to add an app (`AppOnboardingWizard.tsx:294-300`).

**Alex (power user):** Zero keyboard shortcuts; no bulk actions on ManageListings or the admin review queue; the idea→app path forces PRD → designs → cost-estimate serially with no "just convert it" override (`IdeaDetailPage.tsx:203-225`); `window.confirm` dialogs feel like 2010.

**Sam (screen reader / keyboard):** Can tab **out** of every dialog (no focus trap in `ui/dialog.tsx`); can't dismiss 5 modals with Esc (worst: the image lightbox); can't distinguish paid/unpaid stakes or connection status conveyed by colored dots alone; will hit the unlabeled `MoreHorizontal` button in SocialHubLayout as "button".

## 6. What's Working (preserve these)

1. **The money path is real.** Campaign invest → Paystack redirect → sessionStorage round-trip → server verification → portfolio, with a double-charge guard ("do not pay again") and versioned agreement acknowledgement capture. This is the hardest part of a marketplace and it's genuinely built.
2. **Revenue verification discipline.** Listings hide revenue until review approval; badges are earned, not decorative. Extend this ethos to the two fabricated-content spots and the product is coherent.
3. **Navigation integrity.** Zero dead links across 73 routes, a real 404, back-affordances on all 10 detail pages, clean auth-redirect round-trip with post-auth return.
4. **Token/dark-mode architecture.** Semantic tokens are well adopted; the dark-mode pitfall is documented in-code and avoided; reduced-motion has a global block plus `useReducedMotion` in AnimatedIcon.

5. **Wizard draft persistence** (sessionStorage autosave ×3 wizards).

8. **Cleanup** — delete/wire the 4 orphan pages, wire or remove `/app/:id` and `/blog/category/:category`.
